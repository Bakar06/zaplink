Privacy Policy

Zaplink

Effective date: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zaplink ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use the app.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

The App is a puzzle game. The following information is stored on your device using Apple's standard local storage and is not sent to any server we control:

  • Your game progress (completed levels, stars, unlocked chapters)
  • Your Zap (in-game currency) balance and daily ad and hint counters
  • Your sound and display preferences

2. iCloud Sync

To keep your progress when you change or reinstall your device, the App stores the data listed above in your personal iCloud key-value storage (Apple's iCloud). This data is stored in your own iCloud account, is managed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and is not accessible to us. If iCloud is turned off for the App, your progress stays on the device only.

3. Advertising

The App displays full-screen (interstitial) and rewarded advertisements provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use information such as device identifiers (e.g. Apple's IDFA), approximate location, and app usage data, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Where required by law (for example, in the European Economic Area and the UK), the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent before showing personalized ads. The App also requests your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before any tracking occurs. You can change your choice at any time in your device's Settings app (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking). If you decline, the game works the same, but ads may be less relevant.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and Google's advertising policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases (Zap packs and removal of ads). Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details.

5. No Account or Personal Data Collection

The App does not require account creation and does not knowingly collect your name, email address, or other directly identifying personal information.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

sbmlabs.info@gmail.com

1. Cihazınızda Saklanan Bilgiler

Uygulama bir bulmaca oyunudur. Aşağıdaki bilgiler Apple'ın standart yerel depolamasıyla cihazınızda saklanır ve bizim kontrol ettiğimiz hiçbir sunucuya gönderilmez:

  • Oyun ilerlemeniz (tamamlanan seviyeler, yıldızlar, açılan bölümler)
  • Zap (oyun içi para) bakiyeniz ile günlük reklam ve ipucu sayaçları
  • Ses ve görünüm tercihleriniz

2. iCloud Eşitleme

Cihaz değiştirdiğinizde veya uygulamayı yeniden yüklediğinizde ilerlemenizi kaybetmemeniz için Uygulama, yukarıdaki verileri kişisel iCloud anahtar-değer depolamanızda saklar. Apple IDFA), yaklaşık konum ve uygulama kullanım verileri gibi bilgileri kendi gizlilik politikalarına uygun olarak toplayıp kullanabilir.

Yasaların gerektirdiği yerlerde (ör. Avrupa Ekonomik Alanı ve Birleşik Krallık) Uygulama, kişiselleştirilmiş reklam göstermeden önce rızanızı almak için Google'ın User Messaging Platform (UMP) aracını kullanır. Uygulama ayrıca herhangi bir izleme yapılmadan önce Apple'ın Uygulama İzleme Şeffaflığı (ATT) çerçevesi üzerinden izninizi ister. Tercihinizi istediğiniz zaman cihazınızın Ayarlar uygulamasından değiştirebilirsiniz (Ayarlar > Gizlilik ve Güvenlik > İzleme).
